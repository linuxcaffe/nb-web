- The welcome wizard, shown on home:welcome.md. Copied in by nb-web's /setup on a fresh install
  (starter/ in the nb-web repo). Grammar: KIND / TEMPLATE / AT / IF, as in any .{name}-org.md;
  the title placeholder and other builtins are never asked for, so fields have their own names.

## Notebooks
Notebooks for the main parts of your life or work.

### Contacts
People and companies, shown as contact cards: a notebook named contacts turns them on.
> KIND: notebook
> AT: contacts

### Work
> KIND: notebook
> AT: work

### One of your own
A notebook with any name you like.
> KIND: notebook
> AT: {{notebook_name}}

## A first note
A note in home, to start writing.
> KIND: note
> TEMPLATE: first-note
> AT: {{note_name}}
